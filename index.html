What is an owner's representative?

Building something significant should not mean managing it alone.

An owner's representative is your single advocate on a construction project.

Contractors, architects, and vendors each answer to their own firms. An owner's rep answers only to you.

JJG stands on your side of the table, translating the construction world into clear choices and holding every party accountable to what you signed up for, from the first drawing to the final walkthrough.

What does an owner's representative actually do?

An owner's rep manages the architects, contractors, and vendors on your behalf, controls the budget and schedule, and makes sure the finished building matches what you were promised. You get expertise on your side without having to live on the job site.

When should I bring one in?

The earlier the better, ideally before the first shovel hits the ground. The decisions that shape your budget and timeline are made early, and that is when an owner's rep protects you most.

How is that different from my architect or general contractor?

Your architect designs and your contractor builds, and both answer to their own firms. An owner's rep answers only to you, coordinating both and watching the details that neither is paid to protect on your behalf.

What kind of projects does JJG take on?

Commercial and institutional work such as hospitals, hotels, corporate labs, and research facilities, along with large residential developments and private homes where owners want that same discipline applied.

Do I still need one if I trust my builder?

A good builder builds well. An owner's rep makes sure the whole project, budget, schedule, contracts, and quality, serves you. The two roles protect different things.
