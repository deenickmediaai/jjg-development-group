Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

JJG Development Group ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website (the "Site").

1.Information We Collect

Personal information

We collect the following personal information when you voluntarily provide it through our contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your company or organisation
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • The type of project, its stage, and an approximate budget
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in the message field

We do not operate an email newsletter, and the Site does not take payments, so we do not collect subscription or payment information.

Non-personal information

Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. This is used to operate and secure the Site.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use personal data only when there is a valid legal reason to do so, such as fulfilling a contract, complying with legal obligations, or pursuing legitimate business interests. Specifically:

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form

  • To monitor for potential security risks and detect fraud

3.Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: the services that host this Site and deliver contact form submissions to us, subject to their own privacy policies
  • Legal obligations: when required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal processes
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity

4.Cookies and Tracking

This Site does not set cookies, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies of any kind. We do not build a profile of you or follow you across other websites.

The Site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address in order to serve those files. That is the only third party contacted when you simply view the page.

5.Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted once they are no longer needed.
